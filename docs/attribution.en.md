# Attributions and Licenses

The Hapanels project was made possible thanks to the open-source community. Below is a list of projects, typefaces, and graphical assets used in the application, along with information about their authors and licenses.

---

### Source Code and Foundations

- **R1HA**
  The application is based on the R1HA project codebase, which served as the native foundation for Home Assistant integration, WebSocket communication mechanisms, and Jetpack Compose view structures.<br>
  *Project released under the Unlicense (Public Domain).*<br>
  *Link:* **[itskenny0/R1HA](https://github.com/itskenny0/R1HA)**

- **ShellyElevate**
  Hardware solutions, screen brightness control mechanisms, and physical button/relay integration for the Shelly Wall Display are based on technical references from the ShellyElevate project.<br>
  *Project released under the Apache License 2.0.*<br>
  *Link:* **[RapierXbox/ShellyElevate](https://github.com/RapierXbox/ShellyElevate)**

---

### Typefaces (AOD Fonts)

All fonts used in the Always On Display (AOD) clock designs are released under free licenses and obtained from official sources:

- **Monoton, Bangers, Big Shoulders Display, Anton, Orbitron, Cinzel, Playfair Display, Glass Antiqua, Półtawski Nowy**
  Fonts downloaded from the official Google Fonts catalog.<br>
  *Released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.*

- **Zakład Regular**
  An urban typeface inspired by historical Warsaw craft signage, obtained from the Warszawskie Kroje project.<br>
  *Released under the SIL Open Font License.*<br>
  *Link:* **[warszawskie-kroje/zaklad](https://github.com/warszawskie-kroje/zaklad)**

### Graphical Assets

- **Poznań Goats (AOD Graphic)**
  The silhouette of the Poznań Town Hall goats used in the Poznań AOD clock style was created based on publicly available graphical materials, cropped and noise-reduced for display on the panel.
